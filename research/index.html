Research

Systems in Galactic context.

I connect planetary and stellar systems to the Galactic populations they come from, using large astronomical surveys and spectroscopic datasets.

Research Themes Publication List

Research Themes

Exoplanets, substellar companions, multiple-star systems, and compact objects.

01

Planet occurrence in the Galaxy

How metallicity, α-abundance, kinematics, and Galactic component membership change planetary rates and architectures.

02

Planetary system architectures

Metrics for comparing full planetary systems, including the Weighted Energy Distance approach.

03

From planets to substellar companions

Empirical signatures across companion masses, orbital architectures, and host properties that constrain formation theories.

04

Binary and multiple stars

Demographics across Galactic populations, compact hierarchical triples, and tidal circularization physics.

05

Compact objects in survey data

Finding black holes, neutron stars, and white dwarfs in large astrometric and spectroscopic datasets, then using their orbital parameters to probe binary evolution and formation channels.
