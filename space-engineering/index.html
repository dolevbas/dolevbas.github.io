Engineering Work

Spacecraft systems, satellites, and mission payloads.

I am a satellite systems engineer with 15+ years of experience in end-to-end mission practice, spanning concept design, integration testing, launch logistics, and in-orbit commissioning. My PhD background in planetary science and astrophysics quietly guides how I approach complex mission challenges.

My engineering work focuses on spacecraft systems engineering, satellite orbital mechanics, Bayesian statistics, remote sensing, and the effects of the space environment and space weather on mission design.

Previous missions

  • TEVEL-2 - Chief system engineer and integrator; mentored nine high-school teams; flew a dedicated cosmic-ray and radiation sensor payload.
  • TAUSAT-2 - Chief system engineer and integrator of a 2 U CubeSat demonstrating a novel optical LED communications link.
  • TAU COTS-Capsule - Integrator for a radiation-payload experiment flown to the ISS.
  • TEVEL-1 - Mentor for eight high-school teams developing 1 U CubeSats with retro-reflector payloads.
  • TAUSAT-1 - System engineer and integrator of TAU's first 3 U CubeSat, carrying radiation and space-weather instruments.
